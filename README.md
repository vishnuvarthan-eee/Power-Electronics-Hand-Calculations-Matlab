# Power-Electronics-Hand-Calculations-Matlab

Digital repository for power electronics theory, differential equation derivations, hand calculations, and MATLAB/Simulink simulations. Maintained as part of my M.Sc. studies in Power Electronics and Drives at LUT University, Finland.

## Reports & Assignments

### 1. Half-Wave RL Rectifier Analysis
* **Topics Covered:** Mechanical water-wheel inductor analogy, KVL transient/steady-state differential equations ($I_{total} = I_{ss} + I_{dyn}$), extinction angle ($\beta$) derivation, and average/RMS voltage integrals across $20\text{ mH}$, $50\text{ mH}$, and $100\text{ mH}$ loads.
* **File:** Half_Wave_RL_Rectifier_Analysis_Vishnuvarthan.pdf

## Tools & Environment
* **Mathematical Framework:** First-order differential equations, definite integration, trigonometric identities.
* **Simulation Software:** MATLAB & Simulink.
